# Qevli V40.1 — Native Analytics

Adds a first-party admin analytics command center at `/admin/analytics`.

## Included
- DAU / WAU / MAU and new-user counts
- 30-day page-view and session chart
- Platform activity totals for posts, reactions, comments, messages, follows, stories, groups, events, marketplace listings, gaming teams and reports
- Top pages and event names
- Device, browser, operating-system, referrer, language and timezone breakdowns from first-party browser events
- Onboarding acquisition-source breakdown
- Automatic page-view tracking through `QevliAnalyticsTracker`
- Monetization remains disabled

No new external analytics provider is required for these native metrics.
